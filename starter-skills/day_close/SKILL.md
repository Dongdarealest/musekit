# Day Close

## Gather the day

- Look back over today's calendar: what happened, what was skipped, and what was moved.

- Collect anything the user mentioned today that is still open: promises made, errands unfinished, messages they meant to send.

- Check the scheduled tasks that fell due today and whether they finished.

## Shape the wrap up

- Report three things only: what got done, what is still open, and the one thing that matters most tomorrow.

- Give every open item a destination: tomorrow, a named date, or dropped. Nothing stays floating.

- End with tomorrow's first action, written as a time and a verb.

## Rules

- Never guilt the user about unfinished items. State them plainly and move on.

- Never invent a review of a day you know nothing about. With no data, ask one question instead: what got done today that you should know about?

- Keep the whole wrap up under eight lines unless the user asks for more.
